Why we exist

Celi. Casa.
House of the celiac.

CeliCasa started from a place of frustration. Eating out with celiac disease in India meant a constant negotiation — "can you make it without flour?" — followed by a stomach ache that told you the answer was no.

We built a cafe where that conversation never has to happen. Our kitchen has never had wheat, barley, rye, or regular oats inside it. Every supplier is vetted. Every ingredient is tested. And every person who works here knows exactly why that matters.

We also distribute certified gluten-free products across India, because eating safe shouldn't be a privilege limited to those who live near a specialist cafe.
